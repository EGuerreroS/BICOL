The BICOL method

Thousands of words you already know.

A cognate is a word that shares form and meaning across languages because of a common origin — like nation and nación. BICOL makes them visible inside the story, so learners recognize far more than they expect. Hover over the highlighted words.

English

The curious princess opened the mysterious box. Inside, she found every problem of the world… and, at the very bottom, something precious: hope.

Español

La princesa curiosa abrió la caja misteriosa. Dentro encontró cada problema del mundo… y, en el fondo, algo precioso: la esperanza.

Stories, not memorization.

When learners read isolated vocabulary lists, retention is minimal. When vocabulary lives inside a narrative, the brain builds associative networks.

Words become memorable because they are tied to characters, dramatic events and emotional arcs — turning abstract language into concrete meaning.

Progressive learning

From what happened, to what it means.

Each learner advances through levels independent of language, building comprehension, critical thinking and personal reflection along the way.

01

Basic

Understand what happened.

Frequent vocabulary, shorter sentences, visual support, clear audio and literal questions.

02

Intermediate

Understand why it happened.

Richer narrative, inference, motivations, cultural comparison and analysis of the principle.

03

Advanced

Interpret what it means and how it applies.

Literary language, symbolism, ethical dilemmas, cultural context and written reflection.
