Academic partnership

Built with research, not just code.

BICOL's platform is being developed with the Master's in Software Engineering at the Universidad de Guadalajara, Centro Universitario de los Valles (CUValles), through complementary graduate theses that converge in a single product.

1

Architecture & backend

A data model where stories, languages, audio and cognates live as connected objects — ready to scale from families to institutions.

2

Bilingual experience & UX

A synchronized reader that shows two languages at once, aligns text with audio and keeps cognitive load low.

3

Learning analytics & AI

Meaningful progress indicators and dashboards for learners and families, and adaptive story recommendations.
